## Why the detail was safe to drop

Nothing was lost, because the changelog was carrying something that is not its job. The reasoning
behind each change — what was measured, what was rejected, what the failure looked like — lives in
`.brain/changes/`, one note per PR, linked to its ADR and its roadmap track

changelog answers "what changed and does it affect me"; the vault answers "why, and what did we
learn". Duplicating the second into the first is what grew this section to the point where its own
breaking change was invisible.

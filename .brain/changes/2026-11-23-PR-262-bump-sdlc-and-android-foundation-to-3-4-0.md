## Summary

Release-prep version bump: `sdlc` and `android-foundation` go from 3.3.1 to 3.4.0, both minor.
#258 and #260 changed both plugins' content but left their versions at 3.3.1. The `/release`
per-plugin check flagged this before marketplace v4.4.0 was cut. Unlike #256, this bump landed
*before* the release, so v4.4.0 shipped with versions that match its content. Installed copies of
an explicitly versioned plugin may not pick up new content while the version stays the same.

- **What moved.** The two source `plugin.json` files and their emitted `dist/antigravity` copies
  (ADR-0029 emit parity), plus one line in `CHANGELOG.md` `[Unreleased]`, which became the lead line
  of the `[4.4.0]` section.
- **What did not.** The android-foundation manifest's compatibility floors stay as they are. The new
  `role_expertise.project-researcher` block names a role that only exists in sdlc 3.4.0+, but an
  older sdlc never resolves that role, so it simply ignores the block.
- **Why minor.** New user-facing surface:
  - sdlc: the `/sdlc:brief` skill, the `/sdlc:start --brief` flag, the `/sdlc:ask-me` command and
    the read-only `project-researcher` agent;
  - android-foundation: an expertise block and a narrower lint-config guard.

## Decisions & rationale

**The measurement inverted the item's own premise, so nothing was cut.** H5 was written to reduce
prompt volume for cost — decompose `pipeline-orchestrator/SKILL.md`, turn prose into commands, move
fragments out of the stable prefix. The arithmetic says cost is not the argument. A second
measurement taken alongside it says something else is. Recording that without acting on it is the
point of the note: the status is `measured`, not `decided`.

**Compute the saving, do not benchmark it.** Track H's own instruction, and it is what made this
cheap enough to take before committing to any design. The mechanism is deterministic — a token
removed from the stable prefix is not billed on every turn — so the saving is
`removed_prefix_tokens × main_loop_turns × cached_input_price`, arithmetic rather than an
experiment. Benchmarking it would have been actively worse than useless: the run-to-run noise floor
(55.6–64.2% spread on total cache-read, [[planning/backlog]]) swallows the effect entirely. This is
the same lesson [[architecture/benchmark-e2-read-discipline]] paid for in full — a 20-run A/B that measured
noise.

**Token counts are stated as a `chars/4` estimate, not dressed up as exact.** No machine holds the
real token count for a prompt file. Saying so in the note is cheaper than someone later discovering
the precision was invented — and it is the same discipline
[[decisions/ADR-0012-unpriced-runs-must-not-render-a-cap-verdict]] applies to cost: a number whose
basis is unavailable must not be rendered as though it were known.
